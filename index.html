Photo Booth Rental FAQs

How much does a photo booth rental cost?

Surfside Snaps offers a 3-hour package for $150, a 4-hour package for $200, and a 5-hour package for $230. Additional hours are $50.

What is included in a photo booth rental?

Every package includes an iPad photo booth, unlimited photos, a custom photo template, instant sharing by text and email, and fun props.

Can I rent a photo booth for a wedding or birthday?

Yes. Surfside Snaps offers photo booth rentals for weddings, birthdays, private parties, corporate events, schools and graduations.
